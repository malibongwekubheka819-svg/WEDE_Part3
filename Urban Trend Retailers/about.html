Brief History

Urban Trend Retailers was launched in 2018 as a single specialty store in Johannesburg. It specializes in sustainable streetwear, footwear, and accessories.

Driven by passion for fashion and environmental awareness, Urban Trend Retailers has since grown into a well-known specialty retailer with five retail stores nationwide across South Africa.

Brand Insights & Accordions

Launched in 2018 as a single boutique store in Johannesburg, Urban Trend Retailers has expanded to 5 nationwide locations dedicated to sustainable street fashion.

We craft high-quality apparel while systematically lowering the ecological footprint of urban fashion commerce across Southern Africa.
